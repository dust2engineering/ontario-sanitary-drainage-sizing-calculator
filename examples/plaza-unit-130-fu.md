# Example 2 — Two-storey plaza unit, 130 fixture units, 70 FU from one storey

- Branch: NPS 4 (180 FU)
- Stack: NPS 4 holds 540 FU total and 100 FU from one storey -> NPS 4
- Building drain at 1 in 50: NPS 4 holds 240 FU -> NPS 4; at 1 in 100 it holds 180 FU -> still NPS 4.
If the load were 200 FU at 1 in 100, the drain would step up to NPS 5 (390 FU).
